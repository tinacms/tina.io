We're bringing Team and Team Plus together into one TinaCloud Team plan, with Editorial Workflow included at $29 per project per month when billed monthly.

We're giving the pricing page a tidy-up, too. The goal is a little less llama drama when you're comparing what each plan includes, starting with visual editing and unlimited collections on Free.

These changes are still in preview. The launch date is yet to be confirmed.

## Team and Team Plus join the same herd

Today, Team and Team Plus are separate plans. We're combining them into Team and bringing Editorial Workflow along with them. It's currently listed on Team Plus.

If you'd like a teammate to check your content before it reaches your site, [Editorial Workflow](https://tina.io/docs/tinacloud/editorial-workflow) gives you that review step. Editors save changes to a separate branch, and Tina creates a draft pull request. Your team can review the changes before merging them into a protected branch.

Here's the proposed Team pricing:

- $29 per project per month when billed monthly.
- $290 per project per year when billed annually.

All prices are in USD. If you choose annual billing, you'll pay $290 for the year. That works out to about $24.17 a month; the pricing preview rounds the monthly equivalent to $24.

## Free gets a proper spot in the lineup

Free is taking its place alongside the paid plans in the comparison. We're spelling out what's included: visual editing, unlimited projects and unlimited collections. Collections are your content types, such as blog posts or pages.

Those capabilities are already included; the page is catching up. Each paid plan then shows what it adds. Team and Business are billed per project; Enterprise covers the whole organisation.

The updated comparison also spells out the three standard roles on Free, Team and Business: Owner, Admin and Editor. Enterprise would list unlimited roles, marked Beta.

## Let's put bandwidth on the page, too

The preview shows how much bandwidth comes with each plan per month:

| Plan | Proposed bandwidth per month |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 100 GB |
| Team | 500 GB |
| Business | Unlimited |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

Unlimited collections and unlimited projects on Free don't make every allowance unlimited. Bandwidth and user allowances are listed separately, so check those when you're choosing a plan.

Business is still listed at $299 per project per month when billed monthly, or $2,990 per project per year when billed annually. Enterprise keeps custom pricing for the whole organisation.

## For the existing herd

If you're on Team, you'll gain Team Plus benefits, including Editorial Workflow, as part of the combined Team plan.

If you're on Team Plus, you'll receive a coupon to upgrade to Business for 12 months at your current Team Plus price. You can also choose to downgrade at any time.

We'll email you about the changes relevant to your plan. The launch date is still to be confirmed.

You can see the plans available today on the [TinaCloud pricing page](https://tina.io/pricing). If you'd like a hand with your subscription, [email the Tina team](mailto:info@tina.io).
