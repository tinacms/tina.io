We're bringing Team and Team Plus into one herd: a single TinaCloud Team plan with Editorial Workflow. The proposed price is $29 per project per month, or $290 per project per year. All prices are in USD.

The changes are still in preview, and the launch date is yet to be confirmed. Here’s what’s proposed, including what it means if you already have a TinaCloud subscription.

## One Team plan, with Editorial Workflow

The proposed Team plan brings together the current Team and Team Plus plans. Editorial Workflow, currently listed with Team Plus, will be included with Team. It gives editors a review step before changes are merged into a protected branch. [Read how Editorial Workflow works](https://tina.io/docs/tinacloud/editorial-workflow).

Team would cost $29 per project per month with monthly billing, or $290 per project per year with annual billing. The annual charge works out to about $24.17 per month; the pricing preview rounds that monthly equivalent to $24.

Business would cost $299 per project per month, or $2,990 per project per year. Enterprise pricing remains custom and covers the whole organisation.

Unlimited collections and unlimited projects on Free do not mean unlimited usage across every part of the service. The comparison lists monthly bandwidth separately: 100 GB on Free, 500 GB on Team, and unlimited on Business and Enterprise.

## What existing customers can expect

If you’re on Team today, Editorial Workflow will be included in Team at no extra cost. Your subscription price will stay the same.

If you’re on Team Plus and your actual usage fits the new Team plan, you’ll move to Team and pay less from your next billing cycle. You won’t need to take any action.

If your Team Plus usage exceeds the new Team plan, Tina will provide personalised support with a coupon for Business. The support period is still to be confirmed. **[DRAFT PLACEHOLDER: remainder of your current subscription]** is a placeholder for the proposed coupon period, not a confirmed term.

We’ll share more details when the launch date and customer-specific billing changes are confirmed. Until then, you can review the [TinaCloud pricing preview](https://tina-io-git-feature-pricing-page-decisions-tinacms.vercel.app/pricing) for the proposed plan comparison.
