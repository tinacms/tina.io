We're planning to bring Team and Team Plus together into one TinaCloud Team plan, with Editorial Workflow included at $29 per project per month when billed monthly.

The pricing page is also getting a clearer comparison of what each plan includes, starting with visual editing and unlimited collections on Free. These changes are in preview, with the launch date still to be confirmed.

## One Team plan with Editorial Workflow

Today, TinaCloud has separate Team and Team Plus plans. The proposed Team plan brings them together and includes Editorial Workflow, which is currently listed on Team Plus.

If your content needs a review before it reaches your site, [Editorial Workflow](https://tina.io/docs/tinacloud/editorial-workflow) lets editors save changes to a separate branch and creates a draft pull request. Your team can review those changes before merging them into a protected branch.

Under the proposed pricing, Team would cost:

- $29 per project per month when billed monthly.
- $290 per project per year when billed annually.

All prices are in USD. The annual option works out to about $24.17 per month, paid as a yearly charge. The pricing preview displays that monthly equivalent rounded to $24.

## A clearer view of Free

The new comparison puts Free alongside the paid plans and explicitly lists visual editing, unlimited projects and unlimited collections. Collections are your content types, such as blog posts or pages.

These are clarifications on the pricing page, rather than an announcement that those capabilities are newly available. Paid plans build on that starting point, with the comparison showing what each adds. Team and Business are billed per project; Enterprise covers the whole organisation.

The proposed comparison also lists three standard roles on Free, Team and Business: Owner, Admin and Editor. Enterprise would list unlimited roles, marked Beta.

## Bandwidth shown for every plan

The preview adds monthly bandwidth allowances to the plan comparison:

| Plan | Proposed bandwidth per month |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 100 GB |
| Team | 500 GB |
| Business | Unlimited |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

Unlimited collections and unlimited projects on Free do not mean unlimited usage across every part of the service. Bandwidth and user allowances are separate parts of the comparison.

Business remains listed at $299 per project per month when billed monthly, or $2,990 per project per year when billed annually. Enterprise keeps custom pricing for the whole organisation.

## If you're already using TinaCloud

Existing Team customers will gain Team Plus benefits, including Editorial Workflow, as part of the combined Team plan.

Existing Team Plus customers will receive a coupon to upgrade to Business for 12 months at their current Team Plus price. They can also choose to downgrade at any time.

We'll email existing customers about the changes relevant to their plan. The launch date is still to be confirmed.

For the plans available today, see the [current TinaCloud pricing page](https://tina.io/pricing). If you have a question about your subscription, [email the Tina team](mailto:info@tina.io).
