We're bringing Team and Team Plus into one herd: a single TinaCloud Team plan with Editorial Workflow. The price will be $29 per project per month, or $290 per project per year. All prices are in USD.

Here’s what’s changing and what it means if you already have a TinaCloud subscription.

## One Team plan, with Editorial Workflow

The new Team plan brings together the current Team and Team Plus plans. Editorial Workflow, currently listed with Team Plus, will be included with Team. It gives editors a review step before changes are merged into a protected branch. [Read how Editorial Workflow works](https://tina.io/docs/tinacloud/editorial-workflow).

Team will cost $29 per project per month with monthly billing, or $290 per project per year with annual billing. The annual charge works out to about $24.17 per month; the annual billing screenshot below rounds that monthly equivalent to $24.

Business will cost $299 per project per month, or $2,990 per project per year. Enterprise pricing remains custom and covers the whole organisation.

## What existing customers can expect

If you’re on Team today, you’ll gain Editorial Workflow. Your price and billing cycle will stay the same, and you won’t need to take any action.

If you’re on Team Plus and your actual usage fits the new Team plan, your subscription will downgrade to Team from your next billing cycle. You’ll pay less and keep the features you use today. You won’t need to take any action.

If your Team Plus usage exceeds the new Team plan, we’ll upgrade you to Business with a coupon and email you the details. The coupon period is still to be confirmed. **[DRAFT PLACEHOLDER: remainder of your current subscription]** is a placeholder for that period, not a confirmed term.

For this group, automatic renewal will be turned off because you haven’t agreed to pay the Business plan rate. Your subscription will end when the coupon expires. To stay on a paid plan after that date, you’ll need to choose a new subscription. We’ll send reminders as the end date approaches, with a link to choose your subscription and a way to ask us for help.

We’ll email existing subscribers with the details for their account. Questions about your plan? [Talk to the Tina team](mailto:info@tina.io).
