We're simplifying things with TinaCloud and combining the Team and Team Plus into one herd: a single TinaCloud `Team` plan that brings more features to more users. This is more for less - and no one comes out disadvantaged 💪

This plan includes the flagship [Editorial Workflow](https://tina.io/docs/tinacloud/editorial-workflow) feature and is priced per project.

The Team plan will remain at USD $29 per month (or USD $290 per project per year).

## More Info

Here’s what’s changing and what it means if you already have a TinaCloud subscription.

### One Team plan, with Editorial Workflow

The new Team plan brings together the current Team and Team Plus plans. Editorial Workflow, currently listed with Team Plus, will be included with Team.

The [Editorial Workflow](https://tina.io/docs/tinacloud/editorial-workflow) is a fan favourite for content managers who want to ensure all changes have a second set of eyes. It gives editors a branch and review step before changes are merged into a protected branch.

### Bandwidth Support

On the TinaCloud Free plan, unlimited collections and unlimited projects do not mean unlimited usage across every part of the service. The comparison lists monthly bandwidth for TinaCloud usage separately: 100 GB on Free, 500 GB on Team, and unlimited on Business.
