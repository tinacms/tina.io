TinaCMS has some exciting news for our herd!🦙

Since SSW acquired TinaCMS, the 2 most common questions we've received are:

1. Do we have plans to increase the pricing?
2. How can I get the cool Editorial Workflow feature?

So, let's answer both now with some good news!  

🎉 The TinaCMS team are at Copenhagen Developers Festival (aka NDC Copenhagen) and have unveiled 2 game-changing pricing updates - reducing the cost and bringing the flagship feature, Editorial Workflow, to more users.

First up, we’ve made TinaCMS more affordable - TinaCMS's **Business Plan** changed from $599/month to just $299/month, making it easier for businesses to scale their content operations.

We’ve come up with a perfect solution for mid-sized teams by introducing a brand-new pricing tier: **Team Plus**.
For only $49/month, you’ll gain access to our Editorial Workflow feature, previously locked behind the more expensive Business and Enterprise plans.
Now, more users can experience seamless content collaboration ✨

We expect these updates will empower teams of all sizes, providing a better content management workflow.

See the new pricing plans at [tina.io/pricing](https://tina.io/pricing/ "TinaCMS Pricing")
